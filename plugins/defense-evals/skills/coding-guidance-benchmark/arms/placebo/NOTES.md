# Notes on the office plants

These notes are a placebo. They have nothing to do with software, and nothing in them is
meant to change what a coding agent writes.

- The fern by the window is watered on Mondays and Thursdays.
- The rubber plant is turned a quarter of the way round each week so that it grows straight.
- The cactus on the shelf is watered once a month, and less in winter.
- Dead leaves go in the green bin by the door, not in the paper bin.
- If the soil is still damp two fingers down, skip that watering.
